### 30.1 Rarity Weight Nerf (`kubejs/data/apotheosis/rarities/*.json`)

| **ascent** | 100→**220** | 300→**500** | 500→**250** | 100→**30** | 0→0 |
| **summit** | 0→**60** | 120→**350** | 290→**400** | 540→**180** | 50→**10** |
| **pinnacle** (max) | 0→0 | 0→**100** | 100→**350** | 650→**450** | 250→**100** |

### 30.2 Winged Affix Nerf (`kubejs/data/apotheosis/affixes/armor/attribute/winged.json`)

Creative flight chestplate affix hardened:
*   Weight: 25 → **3** (8x rarer)
*   Removed from `epic` — **mythic-only**
*   Affix type: `stat` → `ability` (counts as ability slot)

### 30.3 Potion Charm Endgame Recipe (`kubejs/data/apotheosis/recipe/potion_charm.json`)

**Before (vanilla)** : 6 Blaze Powder + 3 Potions (trivial)
**After** :
```
HSH   H = Mekanism HDPE Sheet × 4
PRP   R = Arcadia Rune Matrix (endgame bridge)
HTH   S = Source Gem · P = Potion × 2 · T = Ghast Tear
```
Charm crafting now requires endgame mastery of Mekanism + Create + TFMG + Ars Nouveau + Apotheosis.

### 30.4 HDPE Sheet Endgame Recipe (in `recipe_overhaul.js` section 13)

**Before (vanilla)** : 3 HDPE Pellets → 1 Sheet via Mekanism Enrichment Chamber
**After** : Create Sequenced Assembly, **5 loops × 6 steps**:
1.  Deploy HDPE Rod
2.  Deploy Brass Sheet (Create)
3.  Deploy TFMG Heavy Plate
4.  Deploy Alloy Reinforced (Mekanism, = enriched_alloy + diamond)
5.  Filling 500 mB Molten Steel (TFMG)
6.  Pressing

**Per sheet cost** : 1 pellet + 5 rods (= 20 pellets) + 5 brass sheets + 5 heavy plates + **5 alloy reinforced (= 5 diamonds)** + 2500 mB molten steel. Total ≈ **21 pellets + 5 diamonds** per sheet.

**HDPE Rod** also hardened : 4 pellets + 1 Brass Sheet (was 4 pellets alone).
