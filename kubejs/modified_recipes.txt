## 9. FUSION CORE CHAIN (5 tiers, 20 items)

**Tier 0** — Alloy Blend, Diamond Matrix, Infused Steel, Nether Concentrate, Energized Dust, Wiring Bundle
*   Create Mixing (heated) with massive resource inputs. Output 1 per craft.

**Tier 1** — Refined Alloy Ingot, Hardened Steel Compound, Energized Crystal, Treated Composite Plate
*   Create Mixing + Mechanical Crafting 5x5. Each consumes 16-26 tier 0 items.

**Tier 2** — Quantum Circuit, Plasma Cell, Reinforced Casing, Thermal Conductor
*   Mechanical Crafting 5x5 + Sequenced Assembly (8 loops).

**Tier 3** — Fusion Matrix (7x7), Containment Field Generator (7x7), Neutron Reflector (10 loops)
*   Requires Nether Stars, Netherite Blocks, all tier 2 items.

**Final** — **Fusion Core** (Mechanical Crafting 9x9)
*   Cost: ~100K Iron, ~50K Diamonds, ~20K Gold, ~10K Netherite
